Open your Image Recognition folder

Choose your Image Recognition folder (the one containing Videos). The tuner lists every processed video in Videos, and uses pillar_mask.png and hsv_settings.json from the Image Recognition folder. Results are saved to Results.

Open folder (Chrome / Edge) can save settings and results straight back into the folder. Choose folder works in every browser, but saves by downloading files instead.
